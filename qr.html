Educational information

Understanding QR codes

QR codes are two-dimensional optical symbols designed to represent information in a machine-readable format. Reliable scanning depends on both the encoded data and the physical quality of the final symbol.

01

Error correction

QR codes contain redundant information that can help a scanner recover encoded data when portions of the symbol are damaged or obscured.

02

Quiet zone

The clear area surrounding a QR code helps scanning systems identify the boundary of the symbol and distinguish it from nearby visual elements.

03

Contrast

Strong visual contrast between the modules and their background generally improves scanning reliability, particularly in printed material.
